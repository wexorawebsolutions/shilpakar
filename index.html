04 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few thoughtful answers.

Does water fasting involve no food? +

Yes. Water-only fasting means consuming water but no food during the fasting period. Seven days of continuous water-only fasting can pose significant medical risks. The seven-day online program describes education and guidance; prolonged fasting requires medical evaluation and supervision, and is not a condition of joining.

Who can join? +

Adults interested in guided fasting education and yoga. Ask a qualified healthcare professional before attempting a prolonged water-only fast.

What is included in my ₹99 registration? +

Access to the seven-day guided program described above. Exact session times, coaching format and community access should be confirmed by the organizer before the page goes live.

How do I receive joining details? +

The organizer should configure their payment provider to collect participant contact details and deliver onboarding instructions after successful payment.
